My Story

I have had the opportunity to experience life in different countries and school systems. I spent most of my childhood in Egypt, where I attended a German school for many years.

In Grade 10, I moved to Germany and experienced living and studying there. Later, in Grade 11, I moved back to Egypt and continued my education at a Canadian school.

Last year, I moved to Munich to continue my journey and study Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at MUDT.

Moving between countries and schools has taught me how to adapt to new environments, meet different people and step outside my comfort zone. It has also made me appreciate my Egyptian roots even more.

My Studies and Goals

I really enjoy my studies, and I always try to do my best and understand what I am learning rather than just memorising information.

I am interested in technology, data and artificial intelligence. My goal is to keep improving my knowledge, develop new skills and make the most of the opportunities I have.
