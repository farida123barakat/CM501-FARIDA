About me

My story

I spent most of my childhood in Egypt, where I went to a German school for many years. In Grade 10, I moved to Germany and lived and studied there. In Grade 11, I moved back to Egypt and finished school at a Canadian school.

Last year, I moved to Munich to study Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at MUDT.

Moving between countries and schools taught me to adapt to new places, meet different people and step outside my comfort zone. It also made me appreciate my Egyptian roots even more.

My studies and goals

I enjoy my studies. I always try to understand what I learn, not just memorise it. I am interested in technology, data and artificial intelligence, and I want to keep building new skills.

Things I love doing

  • Travelling: I travelled a lot as a child and love exploring different cultures.
  • Baking: I love making cakes and trying new designs.
  • Art: I enjoy drawing and painting, even if I am not the best at drawing.
  • Puzzles: they help me switch off and focus. I can do puzzles with up to 5,000 pieces.
  • Summer: my happy place is our beach house on the North Coast in Egypt.

The people who matter most

My family and friends mean everything to me. They support me, make my life more fun and are always there for me.
